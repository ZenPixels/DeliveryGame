Concept art lives in `f:\Projects\DeliveryGame\ArtAssets\` (`companies/`, `characters/`). Hand-drawn
2D character art — the natural style for the planned RPG-style dialog portraits. Surveyed
2026-08-09; " (1)" filename suffixes cleaned off.

**Companies (= the delivery network's fixed points):**
- **Ness Mart** — gas station chain. Logo is a Loch Ness monster swimming through the wordmark;
  teal/red/navy/yellow palette. Pump, storefront, and prop designs exist. **Already placed in the
  test map** (author). PNW-cryptid branding dovetails with the monsters-later story arc.
  (An early brainstorm note also lists "Pump Palace" as a gas station name — possibly Ness's old
  name or a rival chain.)
- **ShellStop** — taco truck ("shell" = taco). Yellow truck, giant taco on top, chalkboard menu,
  skateboarding-taco-with-boombox mascot, merch designs. **Three named workers: Marcello, Siena,
  Emma.**
- **Nucleo** — the island's tech brand: phones and laptops in deliberately bland corporate-flat
  style (contrast with the hand-drawn warmth of the food brands). Billboard designs exist. Obvious
  candidate: the player's phone is a Nucleo.
- **The Bushed Baby** — café/coffee shop; bush-baby (galago) logo with big orange eyes ("bushed" =
  exhausted pun). **Pam**, the barista, is a designed NPC: gives advice or townspeople gossip each
  morning; grew up on the island; talks about how the town has "changed"; believes island
  conspiracies and thinks her manager is "in on it"; *"cafe might be important later on"* (author's
  note). Sample line: "a customer asked for the weirdest thing earlier..." → Pam is the exposition/
  rumor engine and the café is a natural daily-loop hub.

**Characters (roster sheet + individual designs):**
- **MC** — young woman, dark wavy hair, pink beanie, red/pink varsity jacket with patches (tiger,
  feminist symbol, star), freckles, permanently tired deadpan ("blehhhh blehhh"). The exhausted gig
  worker embodied.
- **The arsonist** — pale, unassuming young man, wide eyes, blue polo. Deliberately the most
  ordinary-looking person on the sheet (see [[game-vision]] for his story arc).
- **The Mailman** — older, heavyset, mustached, sweat-stained uniform. **The nemesis arc (author,
  2026-08-09):** the player's gig work threatens his livelihood, so he becomes their antagonist —
  "even though you're really not into it" (the complicity thesis again: you don't hate him, you
  just need the money; both of you are victims of the same economics). He escalates from rival to
  saboteur — **arriving during deliveries to sabotage the player** — and later, as the island
  corrupts, into **some sort of demonic boss**: his personal decline mirrors the town's. Gameplay
  hooks that already exist: his mail truck can be a traffic vehicle with a hostile driver
  personality preset (the personality knobs' first named use); "arrives during deliveries" is a
  stalker-lite encounter director; Pam at the café is the natural gossip channel for his decline.
- **Christian girl** — braids, cross necklace, anxious posture.
- **Cat man** — tall, thin, argyle vest, holds squirming "old cats"; a man who owns lots of cats
  (author confirmed).
- **Baker girls** — two bakers: one quieter (long dark locs), one conversational/social (blonde) —
  implies a bakery location too.
- **Hipster** — glasses, gauged ears, leather jacket.
- **The dog** — whippet-ish dog that orders dog treats (delivery to its collar)... and **eventually
  orders his own dog-to-human translation collar** (author, 2026-08-09). The collar is a DELIVERY
  the player makes: before it he's a dog with strange orders, after it he talks. A character arc
  driven entirely by the delivery mechanic — the template for progression-through-packages.
  Author (2026-08-10): *"maybe the dog comes home with you!"* — candidate for the home-base
  weird-stuff stage ([[game-vision]] day structure).
- **Schiavone family** — a family of **butchers** (author). `.tif` not yet viewed.

**The corruption layer (author, 2026-08-09):** as the town becomes more "evil", the commercial
layer degrades with it — a nice billboard switches to an evil version, the phone's apps glitch into
mild jump scares. "The character of the town gradually changes." Engineering shape: a global
corruption level that swaps branded assets and UI states for corrupted variants — cheap (variant
textures/widgets), high-impact, and it makes Nucleo's bland corporate style thematically loaded.
Every brand asset should eventually get an evil twin.
